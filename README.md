## Featured projects

### [APIS: Adaptive Protective Immune System](https://github.com/shehab6157-design/apis-lateral-movement-detector)

Finds attackers moving between machines inside a network, stolen credentials and hijacked AI agents. It learns what is normal for every device, user and AI agent, and it never acts on one strange signal alone. Tested on real data from Los Alamos National Laboratory, it caught 96 percent of the red team's stolen credential logins and cut false alarms by 79 percent. 236 automated tests.

[Code](https://github.com/shehab6157-design/apis-lateral-movement-detector) and [project site](https://shehab6157-design.github.io/apis-lateral-movement-detector/)

### [Hudward](https://anna.partners/store/@shehab-shibli/hudward)

My anti scam app, built in Anna's Founding Builder Program and live on the Anna App Store. Every day it shows three short rounds, a text message, a social media post and a phone call, and you decide if each one is a scam or real. The Check tab looks at any message you are not sure about: where each link really goes, look alike letters, fake senders and hidden text. It hides codes and card numbers before the AI reads a message, and it never just says safe. Works in English, Hebrew and Arabic.

[Open Hudward](https://anna.partners/store/@shehab-shibli/hudward), [watch the video](https://youtu.be/CFCAFgwjGrA) and [follow it on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@hudward_app)

### [RightPart](https://shehab6157-design.github.io/rightpart/)

Digital work instructions for assembly lines. Every part is known by all of its codes, each step is shown in 3D, a wrong part is stopped at the bench, and the parts list is checked against the instructions. A company can load its own Excel, CSV and PDF files, and the app works offline in English and Hebrew. The demo uses made up products only.

[Live app](https://shehab6157-design.github.io/rightpart/) and [code](https://github.com/shehab6157-design/shehab6157-design.github.io/tree/main/rightpart)

### [Core Defense](https://github.com/shehab6157-design/core-defense)

A security system that helps a small Linux network defend itself, built step by step in my own lab. Small agents on each machine watch logs, network traffic and AI agent activity. No single signal can cut a machine off. Two independent sensors have to agree. Every message is signed, and every action is recorded and can be undone. In tests, 60 prompt injection attempts hidden in logs caused zero harmful actions. In a live SSH attack in my lab, it cut the attacker off with a real firewall rule while my own connection stayed up. 456 automated tests.

[Code](https://github.com/shehab6157-design/core-defense)

### [On-Road Animal Detection](https://github.com/shehab6157-design/on-road-animal-detection-system)

An in car unit on a Raspberry Pi 5 with a night vision camera and a custom YOLOv5 model. It spots animals on the road, warns the driver and alerts other cars within 1 kilometer through AWS IoT Core and Lambda. 83 percent mAP, about 340 milliseconds per frame. This was my graduation project and I was the team leader. Top 100 at Jordan's 12th National Technology Parade.

[Code](https://github.com/shehab6157-design/on-road-animal-detection-system)
